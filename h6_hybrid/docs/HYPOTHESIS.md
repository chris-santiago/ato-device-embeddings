# H6 Hypothesis: Two-Stage Embedding on Hybrid RBA-Derived Dataset

> Pre-registration document. Written before any code is run.
> All design choices are locked. Do not update results into this document — write to REPORT.md.

---

## Claim

Two-stage scoring (Stage 1: set-membership gate → Stage 2: FastText skip-gram mean-pool
cosine distance) on a hybrid dataset (RBA-derived marginals + controlled k=1 country-change
spoof injection) achieves ROC-AUC **greater than** the trivial set-membership baseline
on k=1 (country-change) spoof attacks.

---

## Motivation

H5 stress-tested H2 on i.i.d. synthetic data and found that k=1 (single-feature tz-only
spoof) is decisively below trivial (ROC-AUC 0.530 vs. trivial 0.750). H2 only beats
trivial at k=3. The core open question is whether this failure is a property of the
simplified toy feature space or reflects a fundamental limit of the embedding approach.

The hypothesis: using **real RBA marginals** (empirical co-occurrence distributions of
real login feature combinations) will produce richer, more discriminative embedding spaces,
and the two-stage gate architecture will lift Stage 2 scoring above the trivial baseline
even on the hardest attack type (k=1 country change — VPN-realistic spoofing).

---

## Dataset Design: Hybrid

**Accounts:** Synthetic, with login events sampled from RBA-derived marginals.

**Training corpus:** Each account's login history is generated by chain-sampling from
real co-occurrence distributions extracted from the RBA dataset
(filtered to `login_successful=True, is_attack_ip=False, is_ato=False`).

**Feature correlation structure (chain-sampling):**
1. Device tuple: sample `(os, device_type)` jointly from RBA co-occurrence; then sample
   `browser` conditioned on `os` (i.e., `P(browser | os)` from RBA marginals).
2. Location tuple: sample `country` from the country marginal; sample `region` conditioned
   on `country`; sample `asn_bucket` conditioned on `country`.
3. `rtt_bucket`: sample independently from the marginal (no conditioning).

This avoids implausible combos (e.g., ios + desktop + chrome) while keeping
implementation tractable.

**Attack labels — spoof definition (k=1):**
- k=1: country changed to a **different** country (drawn uniformly from observed countries
  excluding the account's home country); `region` and `asn_bucket` are resampled from
  the new country's conditional distribution. `os`, `browser`, `device_type`, and
  `rtt_bucket` are unchanged.
- This is the honest semantics of a VPN attacker operating from a foreign country — the
  hardest and most realistic spoof case.

---

## Architecture

**Single-stage (baseline):**
- FastText skip-gram (ROBUST_KWARGS from H2 and H2-RBA: sg=1, vector_size=64,
  window=6, negative=10, min_count=1, epochs=20, min_n=3, max_n=6).
- Per-account concatenated corpus (identical invariant to H2_RERUN).
- Anomaly score = cosine_dist(test_event_embedding, account_centroid).

**Trivial baseline:**
- Score = 0 if test device_key (6-tuple of features) is in the account's training
  device set; score = 1 otherwise.

**Two-stage:**
- Stage 1: if known device → score = 0 and stop.
- Stage 2: embedding cosine distance for novel devices only; known devices never
  compete with novel/spoof events.

---

## Success Criterion

**Primary:** Two-stage embedding k=1 ROC-AUC > trivial set-membership ROC-AUC on
k=1 spoofs, with non-overlapping 95% bootstrap CIs (1000 iterations, seed=42).

**Secondary:** ROC-AUC improvement is positive across all k levels (k=1, k=2, k=3).

**Pre-registered contingency (trivial baseline):**
The trivial baseline will structurally be ~0.75 on this hybrid dataset (same mechanical
reason as toy synthetic: known negatives score 0, enrollment negatives and spoofs both
score 1 → 50/50 enrollment/spoof split → AUC ≈ 0.75).

However: if trivial is **not** approximately 0.75 (e.g., it is significantly higher
because enrollment negatives are also novel in RBA feature-space), **do not** declare
failure against a fixed threshold. Instead, restate the finding as "embedding adds X
over trivial" — i.e., measure relative lift regardless of the absolute trivial value.

**Null result:** If embedding AUC ≤ trivial AUC with overlapping CIs on k=1, the
richer RBA marginals do not help, and the k=1 failure generalizes beyond the toy
feature space. This is a valid and informative outcome.

---

## Evaluation Protocol

- N_ACCOUNTS = 400 (matching H2, H4, H5 synthetic experiments)
- N_TRAIN events per account drawn from marginals (target 60, with per-account variance)
- N_SPOOF per account: equal to number of novel negatives (enrollment negatives) for
  balanced k=1/k=2/k=3 comparison
- Bootstrap: 1000 iterations, stratified by account
- Spoof levels: k=1, k=2, k=3 (for comparability with H5 stress test)

---

## Known Risks

1. **Marginal sparsity:** Rare countries may have only a few (region, asn_bucket) pairs.
   Chain-sampling with insufficient observations may collapse to one or two region values.
   Mitigation: add a minimum-count floor (N_MIN_COUNTRY = 5 observations) and fall back
   to country marginal if conditional has < 3 distinct values.

2. **rtt_bucket is below-chance (AUC=0.461):** Established by H3 ablation. Include it
   in the token sequence for fidelity but do not use it as a spoof dimension.

3. **Trivial at k=1 may still dominate:** The two-stage gate result on temporal synthetic
   (H4) was +0.074 GRU over mean-pool. We do not know if mean-pool itself benefits enough
   from richer marginals to beat trivial. The two-stage architecture is the primary test;
   single-stage results are secondary.
